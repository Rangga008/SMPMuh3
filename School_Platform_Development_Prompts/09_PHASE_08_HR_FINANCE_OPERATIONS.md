# PHASE 08 — HR, FINANCE & GENERAL OPERATIONS

## Objective
Expand the operational system into school administration.

## HR
- employee profiles
- teacher/staff classification
- positions
- employment documents
- attendance
- leave if needed
- payroll foundation

## Finance
- income
- expenses
- budget
- invoices
- student billing
- SPP if required
- payment records
- reporting
- audit trail

## General Operations
- school documents
- letters
- inventory
- rooms
- facilities
- agendas
- announcements
- internal records

Do not tightly couple internal finance to ARKAS. ARKAS will be an integration/mapping layer later.

## Done When
Major administrative processes can be managed through the operational platform.
